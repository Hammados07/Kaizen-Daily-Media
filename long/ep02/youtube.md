# Why the clever forecast lost

**Title (under 70 characters):** Why the clever forecast lost | Forecasting explained

**Description:**

Two people guess how many calls a help desk will get next week. One uses a clever method that tracks the growth and the shape of every weekday. The other just averages the last seven days. On one help desk the clever method wins easily. On another, the plain average wins. In five minutes you will know how to tell which one to trust, and why every forecast misses.

The numbers come from the Rimi Lab forecasting lab at its starting values: eight weeks of made-up daily calls, the last week hidden, scored by MAPE (mean absolute percentage error). Average of 7 days 34.9%, weighted average 36.3%, trend + weekly pattern 7.8%. With no weekly pattern and a wobble of 120: 8.7%, 10.7% and 15.8%. Over ten new histories with no pattern, the plain average won 7 times.
