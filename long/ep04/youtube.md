# Cut the price, sell more, earn less

**Title (under 70 characters):** Cut the price, sell more, earn less | Break-even explained

**Description:**

A lemonade stall cuts its price by 10%. It sells 15% more cups. And at the end of the day it has less money than before. Nobody stole anything. In five minutes you will see where the money went, and the one number to check before any discount.

The numbers come from the Rimi Lab break-even lab: rent ₹600 a day, each cup costs ₹8 to make and sells for ₹20, so each cup gives ₹12 and break-even is 50 cups. 100 cups make ₹600 profit. At ₹18 each cup gives ₹10 and break-even is 60 cups. 115 cups (15% more) make ₹550; it takes 120 cups (20% more) to earn ₹600 again. After ₹2 off a ₹20 price, a cup that costs ₹8 needs 20% more sales, one that costs ₹12 needs 33% more, and one that costs ₹16 needs twice the sales.
